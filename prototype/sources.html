Where every layer comes from

Each layer in the time machine, its source, its license and how much to trust it. The ids (G07, G26…) point to the full records in SOURCES.md in the project repository. Nothing on this page is sent anywhere.

Three kinds of picture: measured or mapped data drawn as it is; reconstruction, stylized for positions of plates and continents; illustration where a process is shown but its shape is drawn by hand.
