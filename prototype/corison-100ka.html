What is measured and what is modelled

  • Measured: today's ground (USGS 3DEP via AWS Terrain Tiles, 10 m); which surfaces are Holocene (under ~11,700 years) or older (USGS SIM 2956, Graymer et al. 2007); today's streams (USGS NHD); grape blocks (USDA CDL 2024); the West Napa Fault trace and ~1.3 mm/yr slip (USGS UCERF3); two buried topsoils and rounded stream gravel in the Bale soil (USDA Official Series Description); global sea level (approximate values read from Spratt & Lisiecki 2016, Climate of the Past); oak versus pine swings (Clear Lake pollen core, Adam, Sims & Throckmorton 1981, Geology); the sea entering the Golden Gate ~11,000–10,000 years ago (Atwater et al. 1977, USGS PP 1014); planting 1971 and purchase 1995 (Corison Winery; Vinography 2024).
  • Modelled: how thick each young deposit is (8 m ice-age fill, 3 m late fan, 5 m Holocene, all tapered to zero at the hill front) and when it grew; the Napa River's 6 m ice-age cut; where the old creek channel ran across the property; vegetation colours; the bay drawn from today's depths (ignores the mud laid down since).
  • Not shown: uplift or erosion of the hills (not constrained here), floods year by year, the 1800s farms before the vineyard.
