# 2026-10-09 · mountain-building

- Owner request (project thread, 2026-10-09): use two reference clips (a plate-collision cut-away and a 1.8 Ga plate map) as references only. The globe stays as it is (no flat map). Deep time should outline the continental plates and show them in constant motion. A cross-section should show mountain building where it fits.
- Globe, deep time (older than about 27 Ma): plates are tinted by region and outlined. In the model era (410 Ma to now) colours group EarthByte plate IDs by their leading digit and the outline follows family changes and coasts. Before that, each stylized continent gets its own colour. Comet-like streaks ride each plate and loop without end, so motion reads while paused. Model era: each plate turns about its stage pole from age a to a−5 Myr, taken from the Müller et al. 2019 rotations (SOURCES G32). Stylized era: each continent follows its hand-built path. Speed is exaggerated, as the legend says. Off on the block (`uPlOn`).
- Block: the Plates panel has a second view, "Mountain building" (`mbDraw`). It is a 50 km SW–NE crustal close-up with height above sea level ×2. It shows the Franciscan wedge stacking and rising from the sea (28–9 Ma), magma feeding the Sonoma Volcanics (8.2–2.6 Ma), then the squeeze that lifts and folds the Mayacamas, sags Napa Valley and lifts the Vaca Mts. Erosion fills the valley with gravel. Labelled "Illustrative process · geometry and timing are schematic". The button in the panel header switches views. The view is picked by age unless the viewer chooses (≤ 8.6 Ma: mountains). The panel opens by itself on chapters ch_8ma, ch_3_0ma and ch_1ma, unless the viewer has opened or closed it already.
